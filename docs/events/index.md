title: Overview
---

Starting in 2026, we’ll begin showing up more in person—both by attending conferences focused on open source and technology, and by organizing local meetups and hackathons of our own.

We’re doing this to connect with people who care about building better mobile technology, to learn from the broader community, and to share what we’re working on. Events give us space to exchange ideas, test assumptions, collaborate hands-on, and have honest conversations about the future of the mobile ecosystem.

Some events will be about listening and learning. Others will be more hands-on, bringing people together to build, experiment, or explore new approaches.

Our 2026 event calendar is coming soon, with details on conferences we’ll be attending and community events we’ll be hosting. Check back soon for updates.
