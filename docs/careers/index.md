# Careers at Brax Technologies

!!! info "Community-driven, mission-focused work"
    At Brax Technologies, we are building an open, private and user-first mobile ecosystem, and we believe the best way to do that is by hiring from within our community.

    Whether you contribute code, design, documentation or community support, there are clear paths to turn your impact into paid opportunities and long term collaboration.

## Ways to get involved

- **Bounties**: Paid bounties focused on specific issues in our open ecosystem. Contribute to real work, get rewarded, and make your contributions visible.
- **Contracts**: Longer term, paid engagements for work that goes beyond a single issue. Work on defined initiatives with clear scope and deliverables.
- **Employment**: Full time and part time roles where you join our team in building the future of open, private and user-first mobile technology.

You can explore current opportunities and get involved via our [careers portal](https://careers.braxtech.net).
