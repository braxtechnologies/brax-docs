## 3D model for accessory designers

A detailed 3D model of the **BraX3** is available on Sketchfab:

- [BraX3 3D model on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/brax3-2a512bc6c1b143878153644cdb563fbc)

This model is based on the real device dimensions and is technically sound enough to be used as a reference when you:

- design custom cases, docks or mounts
- verify clearances around buttons, ports and cameras
- prototype accessories before committing to tooling or production

Always validate critical measurements against a physical device or official mechanical drawings when available, especially for tight tolerance parts such as rigid cases or snap-fit mounts.
