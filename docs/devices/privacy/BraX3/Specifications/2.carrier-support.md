Our mission is simple: **to make BraX3 the most accessible de-Googled smartphone in the world**.

Achieving this across different countries, networks, and regulatory environments is a journey—one that comes with unique challenges in every region. Connectivity can vary based on network standards, supported bands, telco-specific restrictions, and approval processes.

That’s why we’re building our compatibility information with **real-world testing and community feedback at its core**.

### **Why Check Compatibility Before Purchasing?**

While BraX3 is designed for **broad compatibility worldwide**, we strongly recommend that you **review the verified carrier lists** before placing your order.
This helps ensure you’ll have the smoothest experience possible with your mobile network from day one.

Our compatibility lists are **community-driven**—they rely heavily on **field testing by our team and our users**.
Because of that, they are **living documents**, constantly growing and improving thanks to your contributions.

If you don’t see your carrier listed yet, it doesn’t automatically mean BraX3 won’t work—it simply means we haven’t had a verified field report for that network yet.

* **Compatibility status** is based on a combination of: Actual user feedback, Official telco documentation or approval, Publicly available network specs, and support information
