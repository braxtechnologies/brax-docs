## 👥 The 4 Management Levels

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)); gap: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 2rem;">

<div style="background: #e8f5e9; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-top: 4px solid #4caf50;">
  <h3 style="color: #2e7d32; margin-top: 0;">🎯 Level 1: Pace Setters</h3>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 0; font-size: .95rem;">
    <strong>Team Leads</strong> who set the pace through example. Lead by doing. Manage 2-5 ICs. Focus: execution excellence, technical mentorship, team productivity.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: #e3f2fd; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-top: 4px solid #1976d2;">
  <h3 style="color: #1565c0; margin-top: 0;">🎯 Level 2: Coaches</h3>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 0; font-size: .95rem;">
    <strong>Managers</strong> who develop people. Coach, unblock, and grow teams. Manage 5-10 ICs or 2-3 Level 1s. Focus: people growth, delivery, culture.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: #fff8e1; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-top: 4px solid #ff9800;">
  <h3 style="color: #e65100; margin-top: 0;">🎯 Level 3: Visionaries</h3>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 0; font-size: .95rem;">
    <strong>Senior Managers/Directors</strong> who set direction. Define strategy, build alignment, manage managers. Manage 10-30 people. Focus: vision, cross-functional leadership.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: #f3e5f5; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-top: 4px solid #9c27b0;">
  <h3 style="color: #7b1fa2; margin-top: 0;">🎯 Level 4: Transformational Leaders</h3>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 0; font-size: .95rem;">
    <strong>VPs/C-Level</strong> who transform organizations. Set company vision, build culture, drive major initiatives. Manage 30+ people. Focus: organizational impact, strategic bets.
  </p>
</div>

</div>

## 🚀 How to Progress as a Manager

<div style="background: #f8f9fa; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid #667eea; margin-bottom: 1rem;">
  <h4 style="color: #667eea; margin-top: 0;">1. Develop Your Team, Not Just Yourself</h4>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin: 0;">
    Manager growth is measured by <strong>team performance and the growth of your reports</strong>. Are people leveling up? Shipping high-quality work?
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: #f8f9fa; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid #764ba2; margin-bottom: 1rem;">
  <h4 style="color: #764ba2; margin-top: 0;">2. Expand Your Scope and Influence</h4>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin: 0;">
    Move from managing a team to <strong>influencing multiple teams or org-wide initiatives</strong>. Drive cross-functional alignment.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: #f8f9fa; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid #667eea; margin-bottom: 1rem;">
  <h4 style="color: #667eea; margin-top: 0;">3. Demonstrate Strategic Thinking</h4>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin: 0;">
    Show you can <strong>set direction, anticipate problems, and make long-term bets</strong> — not just react to immediate needs.
  </p>
</div>

<div style="background: #f8f9fa; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 4px solid #764ba2; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
  <h4 style="color: #764ba2; margin-top: 0;">4. Build and Model Culture</h4>
  <p style="line-height: 1.6; margin: 0;">
    Higher-level managers shape culture. Do you embody transparency, accountability, and trust? Do your teams?
  </p>
</div>
