# Business Units

## Overview

The Brax ecosystem consists of multiple business units working collaboratively to advance our mission of privacy-first, open-source technology. Each business unit operates independently while sharing our core values and commitment to user freedom.

These units include product and platform development organizations, distribution and post-sale service entities, and a non-profit foundation focused on education.

---

## Our Business Units

### Brax Technologies
Our public benefit corporation dedicated to product design, management and marketing.

**Focus Areas:** industrial product design, OS development, app development, community and marketing management.

### Lunr Technologies
Dedicated to device manufacturing and quality control.

**Focus Areas:** prototype development, device manufacturing, project management, quality control, product certification.

### Lunaire Direct
A Canadian entity focused on distribution and post-sale service in Canada.

**Focus Areas:** distribution and fulfillment in Canada, p]ost-sale support, warranty handling, and repairs coordination, local logistics and customer service operations

### Lunar Direct
A US entity focused on distribution and post-sale service for the US and international orders.

**Focus Areas:** distribution and fulfillment in the United States, post-sale support, warranty handling, and repairs coordination, international order logistics and customer service operations

### Mobile Marketplace B.V.
A European entity focused on distribution and post-sale service in the European Union.

**Focus Areas:** distribution and fulfillment in the European Union, post-sale support, warranty handling, and repairs coordination, EU customer service operations and logistics

### Lunar Software Solutions
A services entity focused on payroll operations for contractors and freelancers.

**Focus Areas:** contractor and freelancer payroll processing, invoicing, payments, and reporting support, operational administration for distributed teams

### Good Phone Foundation
A non-profit entity focused on generating and promoting educational content.

**Focus Areas:** educational content creation and publishing, digital literacy and privacy education initiatives, community outreach and public-interest programs

## Collaboration

Our business units work together to:

- **Share Resources**: Technical expertise, infrastructure, and knowledge
- **Coordinate Development**: Aligned roadmaps and compatible technologies
- **Support Community**: Joint community initiatives and open-source contributions
- **Maintain Standards**: Consistent quality and privacy standards across all products
